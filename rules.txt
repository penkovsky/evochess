The EvoChess Game
=================

== EvoChess rules

0. === Chess-Based Rules
   - Pieces move as in chess.
   - Pawn promotion is as in chess, to own-color pieces only.
   - The double Pawn move and en passant are as in chess.
   - Check, mate, and stalemate are as in chess.

1. === Starting Position
   Each side starts with 8 Pawns and a King on their standard squares.

2. === The First Evolutionary Addendum aka "To Minor Piece Promotion"
   Every 3 Pawn moves earn the right to promote the last Pawn that
   moved to a Knight or a Bishop.

3. === The Second Evolutionary Addendum aka "To Rook Promotion"
   Every 3 moves by a player's own minor pieces earn the right to
   promote the last minor piece that moved to a Rook. This mirrors the
   Pawn rule.

4. === The Third Evolutionary Addendum aka "Rook Charges"
   Every Rook carries 5 charges from the moment it is created, whether by
   the "To Rook Promotion" right or by an 8th-rank Pawn promotion.

   - Only the Rook's own moves spend charges.

   - On reaching 0 charges the Rook downgrades on the same square and turn
     to a Knight or a Bishop, the owner's choice. This is mandatory.

   - The downgraded piece counts towards "To Rook Promotion" but can never
     become a Rook again.

   - Capturing a Rook removes it and its charges.

   - Check, mate, and stalemate are judged after the downgrade, not on the
     position before it.

5. === Clarifications
   - Promotion rights accumulate indefinitely.

   - A right is spent only on the piece that just moved, so only on a turn
     when such a piece moves. Unused rights carry over.

   - Only one piece is promoted per move, however many rights are held.

   - Pawn moves, captures included, count only towards "To Minor Piece
     Promotion". Minor-piece moves, captures included, count only towards
     "To Rook Promotion".

   - A Pawn move that evolves counts as a Pawn move. The new minor piece
     starts counting on its own later moves.

   - Evolution does not cancel en passant. The capturing Pawn takes the
     evolved piece on the skipped square. The right was created by a Pawn
     move. Otherwise a promotion right would double as en passant immunity.

   - Promotion resolves at the end of the move.

   - Reaching the 8th rank forces the standard promotion. No other
     promotion that move.

   - Castling is not defined.

     ==== White Pawn promotion example

          | Move # | Comment
     ======================================================================
     e4   | 1      | A Pawn moves.
     d4   | 3      | Another Pawn moves.
     d5   | 5      | 3rd Pawn move: the minor-promotion right is earned.
          |        | Promoting is optional.
     d6   | 7      | A Pawn moves. The unused right is preserved.
     b4=N | 9      | A Pawn moves and spends the right.
     Nd5  | 11     | A minor piece moves. No evolution.
     a4=B | 13     | The 6th Pawn move earns the right again, and spends it.
